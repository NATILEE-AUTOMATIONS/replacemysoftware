FAQ

Common questions

How much does custom landscape proposal software cost?

Custom landscape proposal software starts at $10,000 as a one-time build. Includes your plant catalog, material pricing, proposal templates, and e-signature. No monthly subscription.

Can I use my own plant catalog and pricing?

Yes. The software is built around your actual plant catalog — the varieties you install, your supplier pricing, your markup percentages. No generic databases. Your plants, your margins.

Does the software generate professional-looking proposals?

Yes. Proposals are branded with your logo, include project renderings or photos, plant lists with sizes and quantities, material breakdowns, phased pricing, and payment schedules. Clients approve and sign online.

Can proposals convert into installation jobs?

Yes. Approved proposals automatically create installation jobs with material lists, crew assignments, and project timelines. No re-entering data from the proposal into a separate system.

How is this different from proposal templates in Aspire or LMN?

Generic proposal tools force your design style into their templates. Custom proposal software matches your brand, your design aesthetic, and your pricing structure. You own the output format.
