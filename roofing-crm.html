FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from JobNimbus or ServiceTitan?

JobNimbus and ServiceTitan are SaaS products — you rent them monthly, you adapt to their workflow, and you never own the software. We build your CRM around your workflow. You own it. No per-seat fees, no vendor lock-in, no bloat from features you don't use.

What does $10K actually get me?

A complete custom CRM built around your roofing workflow. Job pipeline, crew management, material ordering, insurance claim tracking, client portal, QuickBooks integration, invoicing. We scope it together, build it in 4-6 weeks, and hand you the keys.

Can you handle insurance claims and Xactimate?

We build the workflow around how you handle claims. Adjuster meetings, claim numbers, supplement requests, approval tracking, document management. We can integrate with Xactimate or build a simpler estimating tool that fits your process.

What about ongoing costs?

You pay for hosting (Supabase, Vercel — typically $0-25/month for small companies), any third-party APIs you choose to use (Twilio for SMS, Resend for email), and your domain. No per-seat fees. No subscription to us. If you want us to maintain or add features later, that's optional.

What if I want to add features later?

It's your code. You can hire any developer to add features. Or hire us — we'll quote you a fair price. No vendor lock-in, no "premium tier" upsells, no per-seat pricing.

How long does it take?

4-6 weeks from the first call to a deployed app. We scope your workflow in week 1, build in weeks 2-4, test and refine in weeks 5-6. You get 6 weeks of free updates after launch.
