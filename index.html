FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Most builds start at $10,000 flat — one time. No per-user fees, no monthly license. You'll pay hosting (Supabase/Vercel, typically $0–25/month) and pennies per email/SMS. Optional retainer if you want us on call after handoff.

How long does a build take?

2–4 weeks from discovery call to live software, including a 6-week window of free updates while you shake it down with your team. AI agents write the code in hours; the calendar is mostly your review time.

Can you replace JobNimbus / AccuLynx / ServiceTitan?

Yes — that's exactly what we do. Your customers, jobs, and history can be exported from your current CRM (we'll walk you through it) and loaded into your new system before launch. Most crews are fully migrated in a day.

Does it really work on phones in the field?

Yes. Every build includes iOS and Android crew apps designed for how field workers actually use phones — large buttons, photo capture, simple flows. No pinching and zooming through a desktop website.

What happens if I stop working with you?

Nothing bad. The code is in your GitHub, the database is your Supabase account, the domain is yours. Hire any developer to maintain or extend it. There is no lock-in — that's the entire point.
