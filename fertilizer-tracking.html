The Problem

State audits are a nightmare. Your tracking system makes them worse.

If you apply fertilizer or chemicals, your state can audit you at any time. No records means fines. Bad records means bigger fines. Spreadsheets get lost. Paper logs get rained on. Here's what you're risking:

State audits without records

Inspector shows up and wants to see application records for the last 12 months. You're digging through paper logs and spreadsheets. Missing records mean fines. Bad records mean bigger fines.

$500-5K per violation

Lost client trust
