The Problem

Every hardscape job you underbid is money out of your pocket

Hardscape estimating is nothing like lawn care. One wrong material calculation on a retaining wall and you've eaten your entire profit margin. Here's what you're dealing with:

Takeoffs take hours by hand

You're measuring paver patterns on graph paper or counting blocks from a PDF. One miscount means you're 200 pavers short on delivery day — and the crew is standing around.

3+ hours per bid

Different costs per job type

Pavers cost different than natural stone. Retaining walls need geogrid. Steps need caps. Your material costs change per supplier, per job — but generic estimating software treats every material the same.

Margin erosion

Estimates don't connect to anything

You estimate in a spreadsheet. Then you re-enter materials into an order form. Then you re-enter job costs into QuickBooks. Three systems, same data, plenty of room for errors.

Dropped numbers, lost profit
The Solution

One estimating tool. Your numbers. No spreadsheet hell.

We build a custom hardscape estimating tool around your actual material costs, labor rates, and supplier pricing — not some generic database that doesn't match reality.

Material takeoff that matches your suppliers

Pavers, wall block, caps, geogrid, fabric, base material — all priced from YOUR supplier lists. Update pricing in one place when costs change. Every estimate auto-updates.

Paver & retaining wall calculators

Input square footage, pattern, and waste factor. Get exact material counts — including cuts, caps, corners, and edge restraints. No more "add 10% and hope."

Labor rates per crew, per job type

Your hardscape crew costs different than your maintenance crew. Set labor rates per crew and job type. Know exactly what every job costs you — not just materials.

Estimate to proposal to invoice in one flow

Build the estimate, generate a client-facing proposal with photos and material specs, then convert to an invoice when the job closes. No re-entering data. No lost numbers.

Job costing & margin tracking
