The Problem

Bad estimates eat your margin before the job even starts.

Hardscaping has tight margins. One wrong takeoff — too much base material, not enough pavers, forgot the edge restraint — and you're working for free. Here's what's happening:

Takeoffs take hours by hand

You're measuring PDFs, counting pavers, calculating base material, looking up wall block specs. 3 hours per estimate. Meanwhile, the client is already meeting another contractor.

3+ hours per estimate

Different costs per job type

Paver patios, retaining walls, walkways, fire pits — each has different materials, different labor, different margins. Your spreadsheet doesn't track that. You estimate flat and lose on the complex jobs.

Margin drift on complex jobs

Estimates don't connect to job costing

You estimate in a spreadsheet. You buy materials from the supplier. You track labor on paper. None of it connects. You don't know if the actual costs matched the estimate until the job is done.

No cost visibility
The Solution

Your estimating tool. Your costs. Your margins.

We build a custom estimating tool around your supplier price lists, your labor rates, and your target margins. Takeoffs in 10 minutes. Every estimate accurate. Connected to job costing.

Your supplier price lists, loaded

Every supplier you buy from — pavers, wall block, caps, base material, edge restraint, polymeric sand. Prices update when your supplier sends a new price sheet.

Your paver patterns, calculated

Your margins, protected
