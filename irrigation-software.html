The Problem

Irrigation isn't irrigation. Stop using software that treats them the same.

Irrigation has challenges irrigation software doesn't handle — zone maps, valve locations, controller programming, backflow certs, winterization scheduling. Generic SaaS lumps you in with mowing companies and misses the parts that matter. Here's what you're dealing with:

Year-round SaaS bills for a seasonal business

Your irrigation season is April-October. But Jobber, Housecall Pro, and ServiceTitan charge you $200-400/month all year. You're paying for software in December when every system is winterized and sitting idle.

$2,400-4,800/year in off-season

No zone maps or valve tracking

A technician is at a property and can't remember where valve #7 is. No zone map in the app. No controller location. They're walking the property for 20 minutes looking for it. Your irrigation software doesn't track irrigation infrastructure — because it wasn't built for irrigation.

20 min wasted per service call

Backflow & winterization scheduling gaps

Backflow testing certifications have expiration dates. Winterization is a 2-week sprint every fall. Spring startups all hit at once. Generic software doesn't track cert renewals, doesn't auto-schedule winterizations, and can't handle the spring startup rush.

Missed revenue, compliance risk
The Solution

One tool for your whole irrigation business.

We build a custom irrigation business tool around your actual workflow — zone maps per property, valve and controller locations, service history, winterization scheduling, backflow cert tracking. Built for irrigation contractors, not adapted from a irrigation template.

Zone maps & valve locations per property

Every property has a zone map with valve locations, controller type, and notes. Technician pulls up the property and knows exactly where everything is. No more walking the property searching.

Winterization & spring startup scheduling

Auto-schedule all winterizations in a 2-week window based on zip code and last frost date. Auto-schedule spring startups when temps hit threshold. Crews get optimized routes for dense irrigation neighborhoods.

Backflow cert & compliance tracking

Track backflow test dates, cert numbers, and expiration per property. Auto-remind clients when certs are due. Export compliance reports for your state or municipality in one click.

Service history & repair tracking

Every repair, valve replacement, head adjustment logged per property. When a client calls about a zone not working, you pull up the history and know what was done last time.

Customer portal

Clients log in to see their service schedule, pay invoices, request repairs, and message your team. Fewer phone calls, fewer "is my system running?" texts.

You own the code

No lock-in. No vendor. If you want to hire a developer next year to add features, you can. The app is yours — we just build it.
