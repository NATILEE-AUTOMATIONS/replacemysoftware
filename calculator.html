Frequently Asked Questions

Is this calculator free?
Yes. No signup, no email, no catch. Use it as many times as you want.
Are the prices accurate?
Prices are based on publicly listed starting rates as of 2026. Actual costs vary by plan, add-ons, and number of seats. Most companies pay more than the listed price after add-ons and per-seat increases.
What does a custom CRM cost?
Our custom CRMs start at $10K — one time. You own the code. No monthly per-seat fees. Optional retainer available for updates and maintenance. Compare that to paying $300–$800/month forever.
What about ongoing costs?
Hosting on Vercel is free for most small businesses. Supabase (database) is free up to 500MB. Email alerts via Resend are free up to 3,000/month. Your only real ongoing cost is your domain (~$15/year). An optional retainer is available if you want help maintaining it.
Can I add tools that aren't listed?
Yes. Use the "Add another tool" field to enter any software name and its monthly price.
